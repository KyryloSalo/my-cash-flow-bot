# Release 0.40.3 — partner access requires a confirmed card

## Verified production scope

The partner-trial hotfix is deployed in production. Registration now reserves a `PartnerTrialClaim` without creating a subscription. Finance access through the PWA, API, and bot—including family, manual, and paid paths—requires a confirmed card. A successful valid PSP bind callback activates the exact server-side snapshotted trial days, including `90` and `7889`; the resulting subscription has `auto_renew=false` and `next_charge=None`. Replay and rebind attempts are protected, existing entitlements are preserved, and cardless legacy claims remain reserved during migration.

This is a documentation-only post-release PATCH. It records the already deployed runtime and does not deploy or redeploy anything.
