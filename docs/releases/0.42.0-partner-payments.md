## Scope

Partner payment reporting adds inclusive Payment.paid_at date filters, daily/weekly/monthly grouping, repeat and renewal payment events, exact currency-separated totals, and durable first-touch registration attribution. The finance-admin report is permission-gated and supports manual partner settlement only; no commissions or automatic payouts are introduced.

Date boundaries are inclusive: 01.01.2026–01.02.2026 includes both dates. Registration before that interval does not exclude a payment within it. Each successful real PAID repeat/renewal event counts separately. Manual, pending, failed, rejected, refunded, zero/blank-currency and canary/internal/test payments are excluded. Totals use Decimal and do not mix currencies. Report access requires both miniapp.view_partnerlink and subscriptions.view_payment.
